ABOUT US

Anime Institute of Technology (AIT) is a creative learning institute dedicated to providing quality education and practical training in Anime, Animation, Illustration, and Digital Art. We aim to help students transform their creativity and imagination into professional skills through experienced instructors, modern tools, and hands-on projects. Our programs are designed for beginners as well as aspiring professional artists who want to build careers in the animation and entertainment industry. Founded by: A team of professional artists, animators, and creative educators. Our Vision: To become a leading institute for anime and animation education. Our Mission: To develop creative talent through practical learning, industry-oriented training, and innovative techniques.
